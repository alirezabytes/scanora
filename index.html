Scanora

Scanora is an accessibility app in active development for Android and Windows. It helps blind and low-vision users read images, printed material, and digital documents with offline text recognition, speech, and audio guidance.

Public installers are not available yet. Browse the source repository for features, build instructions, and ways to contribute.

When you choose the optional Google OCR feature, Scanora uploads a temporary image to your Google Drive, extracts its text, and attempts to delete the temporary file afterward.
