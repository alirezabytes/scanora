Privacy Policy for Scanora

Last updated: November 2026

Scanora is an accessibility app for Android and Windows. It can process photos, documents, and recordings on your device or with a service you choose.

1. Offline use

With Work offline on, Scanora processes supported photos, pages, and recordings on your device. Notes, reading history, settings, saved files, and local error logs stay on your device. Scanora does not send these items to a Scanora server.

2. Google Drive OCR

When you choose Google Drive OCR, Scanora requests the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file.

  • Scanora uses access to files created by the app or explicitly opened with it.
  • Google receives the image and creates a temporary document in your Drive for text extraction.
  • Scanora asks Google to delete the temporary file afterward. If cleanup fails, you can delete the file from Drive.

3. Other online tools

When you choose an online provider, Scanora sends the information needed for your request directly to that provider. This can include an image, a recording, or a question about an image. Provider policies determine how those requests are processed and retained. Scanora stores connection keys in its settings on your device and uses each key to contact the corresponding provider. Opening a web link connects to that website.

4. Your choices

Turn on Work offline in Settings to process supported inputs locally. You can remove saved connection keys, disconnect Google, or change camera and microphone permissions in device settings.
