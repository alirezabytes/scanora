Privacy Policy for Scanora

Last updated: November 2026

Scanora ("the App") is a desktop and Android application that allows users to extract text from images using Google’s OCR technology through the Google Drive API.

1. Data Collection and Use

The App only requests access to the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This means:

  • The App can only access files that the user explicitly creates or opens with the App.
  • The App does not have access to the user’s other files in Google Drive.
  • Images uploaded for OCR are processed temporarily and deleted after the text is extracted.

2. Data Storage

  • The App does not permanently store any user images or extracted text on external servers.
  • Temporary files created in the user’s Google Drive are deleted by the App after processing.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Drive API and Google Sign-In. Use of these services is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
